
# AI-Powered Recruitment Assistant

This project is an AI-driven recruitment solution designed to streamline the hiring process for recruiters by automating resume parsing, candidate screening, interview scheduling, and conducting initial AI-powered phone interviews. With a unique focus on an AI phone screening assistant, the solution saves time, reduces hiring bias, and provides insightful analytics for more informed hiring decisions.

## Features

1. **Centralized Application Tracking**: 
   - Manage and track applications from multiple platforms in a single dashboard.
  
2. **Advanced Resume Parsing**:
   - Uses Gemini AI to parse resumes, extracting detailed information to determine suitability based on job requirements.

3. **Automated Candidate Filtering**:
   - Automatically filters candidates based on job criteria and recruiter-defined parameters.

4. **AI Phone Interviewer**:
   - Conducts AI-powered phone interviews to further screen candidates, asking tailored questions based on job requirements.
   - Provides analytics from phone screenings, helping to assess candidates’ responses in real-time.

5. **Intelligent Scheduling**:
   - Automates scheduling for candidates who pass initial screening and prepares links for virtual interviews on Google Meet, Zoom, or Teams.
  
6. **In-Depth Analytics**:
   - Detailed insights on resume matches, interview performance, and cultural fit to guide recruiters in their final selection.

## Key Technologies Used

- **Dialogflow CX**: Powers the AI chatbot and phone interviewer, handling natural language understanding and response.
- **Google Meet API**: Automates interview scheduling and link generation.
- **Google Calendar API**: Manages interview scheduling.
- **Gemini AI**: Processes resumes, identifies key skills and experience, and matches candidates to job requirements.

## Usage

1. **Resume Parsing and Filtering**: The AI parses resumes uploaded to the system, scores candidates, and filters them based on recruiter-set criteria.
2. **AI Phone Interviewing**: Candidates passing the initial filter are automatically invited to a phone interview with the AI assistant.
3. **Dashboard**: Recruiters can track applicants, review interview analytics, and make data-driven hiring decisions.

## Roadmap

- **Candidate Management**: Enhance candidate tracking and interaction history.
- **Expanded Integrations**: Add support for additional video conferencing platforms.
- **Machine Learning Model Training**: Improve scoring and filtering models based on historical data.
